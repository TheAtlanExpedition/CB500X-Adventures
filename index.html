My Adventures On The CB500X

My first ride on dirt and with street tyres no less.

Blackdown Tablelands,

It was intimidating to start with but I'd already gotten some good advice online about standing, essentailly "stand when you don't want to and sit when it's easy".
The corrogation didn't help with the street tyres and pressures, I was slowing to a crawl around corners to avoid slipping on the gravel, keep in mind this was before I'd gotten any protection for me or the bike.

There were some great views around Blackdown Tablelands, this was on the way up the mountain on the side of the sealed road was a pitstop with some stones arranged for an unofficial camp fire.
At the top of the mountain was an incredible view a short walk from the rest stop just before the road turned to dirt.
